# A negative answer to Delgado, Süer and Yeşil's symmetrization question

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

The surjectivity question in the final paragraph of Section 5, p. 9, of Delgado, Süer and Yeşil's A Symmetrization for Almost Symmetric Numerical Semigroups (arXiv:2611.14268v1) has a negative answer: pseudo-Frobenius row deletion always leaves 1 as a gap. The symmetric numerical set {0,1} union {n >= 4} is therefore not in the image. In fact, at least half of all symmetric numerical sets with any prescribed odd Frobenius number at least 3 are excluded. Every symmetric numerical semigroup, in contrast, is fixed by the map. We identify the entire fiber over the target with sole gap 1 and obtain at most 2^(floor((F+1)/2)-1) distinct images of sources with Frobenius number F.
