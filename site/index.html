Extensions of Ma–Zhang's negative answer to Delgado, Süer and Yeşil's symmetrization question

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 5 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

We extend Ma and Zhang's negative answer (2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24391331, Section 2) to the surjectivity question in Delgado, Süer and Yeşil, A Symmetrization for Almost Symmetric Numerical Semigroups (arXiv:2611.14268v1, Section 5, p. 9, final paragraph): the row-deletion map omits not only targets containing 1, but also some reduced symmetric targets whose diagrams have no full cell below the diagonal. The ordered output elements satisfy b_i >= 2i; equality at b_1 = 2 forces every element below the conductor to be even. The smallest excluded reduced target has Frobenius number 7; under the full-cell restriction the minimum is 9. We give infinite excluded families, retain the exact ordinary fiber and fixed-point characterization, and distinguish fixed-source image bounds from fixed-target counts. For symmetric targets of multiplicity three and Frobenius number 2h−1, there is one image for h=3,4,5 and exactly two for every h≥6. At arbitrary output multiplicity r<h, every source has multiplicity at most 2r−2 and Frobenius number at most 2h+2r−5.
